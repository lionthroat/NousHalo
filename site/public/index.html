Nous Halo

A free drawing program for leaving Illustrator behind. It opens your .ai files with their layers, groups and text, puts hue shifting front and centre, keeps a piece true to its palette, and animates. No subscription, ever.

Using Nous Halo

Open an Illustrator file with File → Open (Ctrl+O). It comes in with its artboards, layers, groups, text and images; save it as a .halo file to keep working on it, or export it again as .ai, SVG or PNG.

Spin the palette

In the Halo panel, every colour in the piece is a dot. Drag one to change that colour; drag empty space to turn them all around the wheel.

Lock to a palette

Choose a palette and tick Lock piece to this palette. Colour pickers only offer its colours, and Conform snaps everything to it, images included.

Build palettes

In Palettes, make one from the document, an image or a colour harmony. Brings in Adobe .ase swatches, too.

Animate

In the Timeline, add frames, hide or nudge layers in each, and Tween the in-betweens. Export PNG frames or a sprite sheet.

Trace a scan

Place a scanned drawing, select it, then Object → Image Trace turns its ink into vector shapes.

Groups and guides

Ctrl+G groups; double-click a group to work inside it. Ctrl+R shows rulers to drag guides from.

F1 in the program lists every shortcut.

Questions

Why does my computer warn me about it?

Microsoft and Apple charge yearly fees for the certificates that make those warnings go away. Nous Halo is free and made by one person, so it skips them. The warnings appear once; after that it opens like any other program.

Is it really free?

Yes, no trial, no subscription, no ads. It's open source: anyone can read exactly what it does on GitHub.

Will it open my Illustrator files?

Yes: .ai files saved with Illustrator's default “PDF Compatible” option (most of them), Illustrator EPS files (Adobe Stock downloads are these, even when they end in .ai) and .ai files from Photopea. They come in with artboards, layers, groups, clipping masks, text and images. Gradients, meshes, blend modes and effects don't come across yet. Fonts you don't have installed keep their look until you edit the text.

Can I send work back to someone who uses Illustrator?

File → Export as Illustrator (.ai) writes the same kind of .ai Illustrator saves by default, with layers and editable text. SVG and PNG export are there too.

How do I get a newer version?

Download and install it the same way. It replaces the old one.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows: Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Nous Halo → Uninstall. Mac: drag Nous Halo from Applications to the Trash. Linux: remove “nous-halo” in Software Manager, or sudo apt remove nous-halo.
